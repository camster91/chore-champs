# ChoreChamps launch announcement draft

## Short version

Meet ChoreChamps: a colorful family chore tracker that turns everyday tasks
into progress, points, and rewards. Parents create chores, approve completions,
and choose the rewards. Kids see what to do, build streaks, and celebrate their
progress.

ChoreChamps is live at [chores.ashbi.ca](https://chores.ashbi.ca). It has no ads,
no subscriptions, and keeps child profiles under parent control.

The iOS and Android store submissions are prepared and awaiting developer-console
setup, signed-build upload, and store review.

## Longer version

Household chores do not have to feel like a daily argument. ChoreChamps helps
families turn responsibilities into a simple shared routine:

- Parents create chores, assign them to children, and set point values.
- Children see their tasks, earn points after approval, and build streaks.
- Families choose their own rewards and manage profiles from one account.
- The responsive app works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

ChoreChamps is designed for parent-controlled family use, with no advertising
and no subscription plan. Read the [privacy policy](https://chores.ashbi.ca/privacy-policy)
before creating an account.

Try it at [chores.ashbi.ca](https://chores.ashbi.ca).
