### Subtitle

### Description

ChoreChamps turns everyday household tasks into exciting quests your kids actually want to complete. Assign chores, track points, and let rewards do the motivating.

**How it works for parents:**
- Create chores and set your own point values for each task
- Add your kids and assign chores individually or to the whole family
- Review completed submissions and approve with one tap
- Set up a rewards store — decide what prizes are worth and let them earn it

**How it works for kids:**
- See their assigned chores in a colorful, gamified dashboard
- Earn points for every completed and approved task
- Unlock badges for streaks and consistency
- Redeem points for rewards you've set — no need to spend real money

**Built for families:**
- Works across all devices — phone, tablet, desktop
- Real-time sync so everyone's always up to date
- No ads, no in-app purchases beyond the initial price
- Family accounts with parent-controlled settings

**Privacy for parents:**
- You control all data. Child profiles are managed by the parent account.
- No third-party analytics targeting children.
- Full privacy policy available.
